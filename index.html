[ RISK.PROFILE ]

Why unified margin changes the risk math.

Nado is the central-limit-orderbook exchange from the team that built Kraken, deployed on Ink — Kraken's own Layer 2. Its defining feature is unified margin: one collateral pool spans spot, perpetuals, and money markets, rather than isolated per-market margin.

What that means for risk

Because collateral is shared across your whole account, a single losing position can pull your entire portfolio toward liquidation — not just that one trade. Nado's own launch language calls it "the perfect storm." That's not just a tagline; it's a fair description of how fast risk can compound in a unified-margin account.

What "buffer %" measures

How much of your account's asset value still cushions you above Nado's maintenance-margin line — (assets − liabilities) / assets. NadoGard reads this straight from Nado's own gateway — the same number the exchange itself uses to decide whether to liquidate you.

Three kinds of alert, one account.

Each one mirrors an actual bot command. No dashboard to check — everything arrives as a message, the moment it's true.

/watch

Liquidation buffer alerts

Set your own thresholds. Get pinged the moment your buffer crosses one, in either direction. Every alert with an open position includes a Close button, built and proven against real orders on Nado's live gateway.

/pricealert

Price & funding-rate alerts

Tell it a market, a direction, and a level. It watches Nado's live orderbook and funding feed and messages you the instant either one crosses.

/digest

Weekly points & rank recap

A standing digest of your Nado points and rank, delivered on its own schedule.

[ SAFETY.LOG ]

Read-only by default.

Watching your account and acting on it are two separate permissions — you decide if and when to grant the second one.

No private key needed for alerts

Watching a wallet only ever reads public position data from Nado's gateway — nothing to authorize, nothing at risk.

Closing is opt-in and encrypted

The Close button only appears once you've linked a dedicated trading-only signer key — stored encrypted, never your main wallet key.

Reduce-only, every time

Every close order is hard-coded reduce-only — it can shrink or close a position, never open a new one or flip its direction.

Confirm before it executes

Tapping Close shows a confirmation screen with your live position and price first — nothing sends until you tap it a second time.
